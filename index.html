Finance in practice

Three questions a board asks

How I use reporting to answer them, shown with a Power BI model I designed, on fictional data for an illustrative €25M ARR SaaS business.

01 · Growth

Is our growth durable?

ARR grew 30% to €25M, and 62% of new ARR came from existing customers expanding.

What it showed
Net revenue retention of 116% and gross retention of 94%, on a customer base that grew by only five logos.
So what
Growth is driven by value per customer. That points investment toward customer success and expansion pricing, with NRR tracked as a board KPI.
02 · Profit

Why did profit dip this month?

Net profit fell €53K below the 3-month average, despite revenue rising €67K.

What it showed
An autumn sales and marketing campaign added €72K of cost. Without it, profit would have been about €19K above trend.
So what
The dip was a deliberate investment, not a margin problem. The right response is a campaign return checkpoint before further Q4 spend.
03 · Cash

Can we fund the next quarter?

Cash stays above €8.6M in every week of the 13-week forecast, more than double the €4M minimum.

What it showed
The low point comes in a payroll week before Christmas. Even in the downside case (late collections, unexpected spend), cash never falls below €7.4M.
So what
Planned hiring can proceed and the credit facility can stay undrawn, with weekly tracking against forecast.
